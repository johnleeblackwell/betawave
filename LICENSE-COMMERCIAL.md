## Why this exists

βWave is free software under the AGPL, and always will be. The AGPL carries one
obligation that many businesses cannot accept: **if you modify βWave and run it
as a service that other people reach over a network, you must publish your
modifications under the same licence.**

For a developer running it on their own box, that costs nothing. For a company
whose lawyers will not permit its source code to leave the building, it is a
blocker — and no amount of "you probably won't have to" makes it acceptable to a
legal department.

The commercial licence releases you from that obligation. That is what you are
buying: **not the software, which is free, but the freedom not to publish your
own work.**

## Tiers

### Standard Licence
For **one organisation**, covering that organisation and the brands it owns.

Grants a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use,
modify and deploy βWave for that organisation's own purposes, **without the
source-disclosure obligations of the AGPL**.

Includes installation on your infrastructure, initial configuration, and 30 days
of support from the install date.

### Managed — $379/month, twelve-month term

For businesses who want βWave running properly without running it themselves.
Installed, configured, maintained and updated on **your own server**, with your
own API keys. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.

**The licence vests.** On completion of the twelve-month term, the Standard
Licence described above becomes yours **permanently and at no further cost**.
From that point you may stop paying at any time and keep:

- the software, on your own server, running;
- all content, data and configuration it holds;
- a perpetual commercial licence to keep using and modifying it.

This is the difference between owning something and renting it, and it is
written here rather than only on the website because a promise a customer
cannot point at is not a promise.

Before the twelve months are complete the licence has not yet vested; ending
early ends the right to use the software commercially, though the AGPL option
remains open to you as it does to anyone.

### Agency Licence
For **agencies, consultancies and service providers** operating βWave on behalf
of clients.

Everything in the Standard Licence, plus the right to run βWave for client
brands, white-labelled under your own name, with each client's data isolated.
Your clients do not need their own licence while you operate it for them.

## What no commercial licence grants

Neither tier permits you to:

1. **Resell or redistribute βWave itself** as a product, whether modified or not.
2. **Operate βWave as a multi-tenant SaaS** offered to the general public — that
   is, sign-up-and-use hosting where the software is the product. Running it for
   named clients under an Agency Licence is expressly permitted; running it as a
   self-service platform is not.
3. **Sub-license, sell, or transfer** the licence itself, except with the whole
   business as part of a sale or merger, on written notice.
4. Remove or obscure copyright notices in the source.

The distinction that matters: **you may use βWave to deliver a service. You may
not turn βWave into the service you sell.**

## Support, and what it is

The 30 days included covers installation problems, configuration questions, and
defects. It does not cover writing your content, running your campaigns, or
training your staff — those are services, priced separately.

Support after 30 days is available on a monthly plan. No plan is required to
keep using the software: **the licence is perpetual and does not lapse.** If you
stop paying for support you keep the software and the version you have, you
simply stop receiving help and updates.
